# R.K. Typing Tutor 🚀

**R.K. Typing Tutor** is a professional, highly accurate, and comprehensive typing practice application built for Windows. 

Designed meticulously for students, professionals, and government job aspirants, this software provides the perfect environment to improve typing speed and accuracy in both **Hindi** and **English**. Whether you are starting from scratch or preparing for a highly competitive exam, R.K. Typing Tutor offers the tools you need to succeed.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. Dual Language Mode (Hindi & English)
- **Hindi (KrutiDev 010):** Master Hindi typing with industry-standard KrutiDev fonts. The app strictly follows real exam typing mechanics, handling complex Alt-codes and special characters flawlessly.
- **English:** A dedicated English typing mode with isolated scoring, progress tracking, and specific exercises.

### 2. Advanced Exam Engine
Simulate real-world exam conditions with our highly accurate assessment engine. The application calculates your results based on standard typing test formulas:
- **Gross Speed (WPM):** Total words typed.
- **Net Speed (WPM):** Actual speed after deducting mistakes.
- **Error Calculation:** Detailed breakdown of Wrong Words, Extra Words, and Total Mistakes.
- **Tolerance Engine:** Built-in calculation for standard 5% error tolerance rules used in various government typing exams.

### 3. Beautiful, Distraction-Free Interface
- Designed with focus in mind, the application features a clean, responsive, and modern UI. 
- Fully supports a beautiful **Dark Mode** for comfortable night-time or low-light practice.

### 4. Offline First & Cloud Sync
- **Practice Anywhere:** R.K. Typing Tutor works completely offline. You don't need a constant internet connection to practice.
- **Safe Syncing:** Your scores, settings, and progress are saved locally on your device and are securely synchronized to the cloud in the background whenever you are online. 

### 5. Historical Analytics
- Track your learning journey with the built-in analytics dashboard.
- View detailed past exam results to monitor your speed progression and mistake patterns over time.

---

## ⚙️ Customization & Settings
Tailor your practice sessions to your exact needs:
- **Timer Settings:** Choose exam durations ranging from 1 to 15 minutes.
- **Start Modes:** Start your exam immediately on a button click, or wait for your first keystroke ('Press Any Key').
- **Visual Assists:** Toggle scroll modes, highlight modes, and backspace restrictions to simulate strict exam rules.
- **Font Sizing:** Fully adjustable font sizes for both original and typed text to suit your screen and visual preference.

---

## 🔒 Security & Integrity
We take the integrity of your typing exams seriously. The application features built-in anti-tamper mechanisms and strict isolation between practice modes to ensure that all scores and progress records are authentic and accurate.
